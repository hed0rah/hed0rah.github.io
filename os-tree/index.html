About OSFAMTRE
▤

OSFAMTRE.EXE   Version 1.00

An interactive genealogy of 120 operating systems, charted across 65 years of computing history.

Each entry is placed on the vertical axis at its year of first release. Click any node to trace its lineage: ancestors highlight in blue, descendants in pink.

Controls: scroll zoom, drag pan, / search, Esc close. Click empty canvas to clear selection. Click lineage swatches to filter.

After chococigar's 2020 diagram, expanded with research on obscure and modern systems.
