Subgenre Map

Nine load-bearing terms. Hover or tap one for tempo range, key labels and a short-list of who to start with.

  • Jungle is the root of the tree: chopped breakbeats over reggae and dancehall bass, 1991 to 1995.
  • Liquid Funk smoothed it out in the mid-2000s with soul and jazz influence.
  • Techstep stripped the soul back out in favor of metallic, mechanical drum programming.
  • Neurofunk is the modern festival-stage default: distorted, triplet-heavy, relentless.
  • Autonomic reacted against all that density with space and minimalism.
  • Drumfunk keeps the drum editing technical and dry, bass mostly out of the way.
  • Jump-Up keeps it simple and built for a crowd reaction over technical depth.
  • Halftime borrows footwork and trap drum feel underneath a full-tempo bassline.
  • Leftfield / Bass-Adjacent is not drum and bass at all, but the same ears keep ending up here.
